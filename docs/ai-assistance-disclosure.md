## Evidence to keep

- Prompts used for substantive generated code/design tasks, if the employer requests full prompt history.
- Actual code modifications and test failures encountered; version history in Git.
- Which parts were AI-assisted versus independently implemented, where known.
- Reports from **your machine**, not assistant-generated hypothetical success claims.

Do not claim the entire codebase was independently written if it was substantially AI-assisted. Do not publish secrets or private conversations containing credentials.
